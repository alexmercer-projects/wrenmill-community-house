What we run

Programs that stay on the calendar

  • Tuesdays and Thursdays

    After-school club

    3:15 to 5:30, for ages 7 to 12. Homework first, then games or art. A snack is included. $10 a week, or talk to us if that is a stretch.

  • Every Wednesday

    Senior lunch

    A sit-down meal at noon, then coffee and cards if you want to stay. We can arrange a ride within Ashford Ferry if you call the day before.

  • First Friday of the month

    Community potluck

    The easiest way to meet neighbors. No program, no speaker, just a long table and enough chairs.

  • By request

    Hall rental

    Meetings, small parties, rehearsal, a memorial. The main hall seats about 80. Kitchen use is extra and we ask you to leave it as you found it.

Using the building

Rent the hall for a few hours or a full day

The main room has tables, chairs, a piano, and a small kitchen. Evening rentals end at 10 pm so the neighbors can sleep. We do not book parties with a cash bar.
